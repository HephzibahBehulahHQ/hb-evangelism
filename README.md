## About HB Evangelism

**HB Evangelism** is the Christian evangelism and digital ministry division of **Hephzibah Behulah**.

We develop digital platforms, educational resources, interactive applications and Christian content designed to help people study the Bible, understand Christian teachings and grow in their knowledge of God's Word.

Our mission is to use modern technology to make biblical learning accessible, engaging and meaningful for people of different ages and backgrounds.

> "Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature."  
> — Mark 16:15 (KJV)

## Our Vision

To build an accessible digital ecosystem that supports Christian evangelism, Bible study, spiritual education and faith-centered learning worldwide.

## Our Mission

To share the message of Jesus Christ through reliable biblical resources, innovative technology, educational tools and interactive digital experiences.

## Our Products and Development Areas

### 1. Christian eBible

A digital Bible application designed for reading, searching, studying and understanding Scripture.

Planned features may include:

- Bible reading and navigation
- Scripture search
- Bookmarks and personal notes
- Reading plans
- Bible study references
- Multiple language support
- Accessibility features

### 2. Christian Educational Games

Interactive games designed to make biblical learning engaging for children, young people and adults.

Development areas include:

- Bible quizzes
- Scripture memorization games
- Bible character challenges
- Biblical history activities
- Interactive learning experiences

### 3. Bible Study Platform

Educational resources for understanding Scripture through structured lessons, references and guided study materials.

### 4. Daily Devotionals

Digital devotional resources designed to encourage regular Bible reading, reflection and prayer.

### 5. Christian Publishing

Articles, Bible studies, educational books and faith-based publications.

Topics may include Christian living, discipleship, salvation, prayer, biblical history and spiritual growth.

### 6. Digital Evangelism Resources

Shareable Christian educational materials and online resources supporting respectful, accessible evangelism.

*These are planned product areas. Availability and development status will be documented individually.*

## Our Biblical Foundation

We believe that Scripture should be approached with care, respect and attention to its context.

Our educational resources aim to:

- Reference Bible passages accurately.
- Distinguish Scripture from commentary and personal interpretation.
- Encourage readers to examine biblical context.
- Respect copyright and licensing requirements for Bible translations.
- Avoid misleading quotations or unsupported claims.
- Present Christian teachings in clear, understandable language.

## Technology and Development Standards

Our applications follow the engineering and quality standards of Hephzibah Behulah.

Development principles include:

1. Keep independent products in separate repositories.
2. Build responsive and accessible interfaces.
3. Protect personal information and user privacy.
4. Clearly identify Bible translations and their licenses.
5. Review biblical quotations and references for accuracy.
6. Provide appropriate safeguards for child-oriented applications.
7. Maintain clear documentation and version control.
8. Test important functionality before deployment.
9. Use approved corporate design standards.
10. Avoid disrupting existing working applications.
